Spellvane — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026 — applies to versions of Spellvane that show ads.

Spellvane is a single-player game published by Hamid Zaib ("we").

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and the game has no accounts.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one to revive or to earn bonus coins). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's ad software in the app collects some device data, described below.
  • There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt a run.

Data collected through advertising (Google AdMob)

When the game loads or shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and send to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device or app identifiers,
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate (city-level) location,
  • device information (model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type),
  • ad interactions and diagnostics (which ads were shown or tapped, crashes and performance of the ad software).

Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see an ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, personalise ads. We (the developer) receive only aggregated statistics such as how many ads were shown; we cannot see who you are.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
 Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some other regions): on first launch you are asked whether you consent to personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings → Privacy options.
  • Android settings: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off ad personalisation, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) on your device.
  • Don't watch ads: every ad in Spellvane is optional. The game is fully playable without watching any.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (hero name, coins, unlocked spells, best distance, daily quests and settings) is saved in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it. If your device has system backup enabled, your device maker or Google may include app data in that backup under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — used only by the ad software to load ads.
  • Vibration — optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID — used by the ad software as described above.

Children

Spellvane is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any personal data about you ourselves. For data held by Google, see Google's policy above; resetting your advertising ID on your device disconnects past ad data from your device.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
